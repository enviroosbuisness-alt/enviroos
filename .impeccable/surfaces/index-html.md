# Surface brief — EnviroOS app interface (canon rebuild)

Scope: full app interface rebuild (home, soil, crop, weather, history, Agri AI, pump/robot, sheets, nav), code-led. Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience/job: average Indian farmer, limited reading comfort, Hindi-first, voice-led; job is "what must I do today" answered in seconds, in sunlight, one thumb, patchy network.
Action/task: check status, listen to advice, ask Agri aloud, approve or adjust automatic pump watering.
Proof/content: real sensor ranges, real i18n copy, real captured Agri answer, working offline/demo data. No fabricated claims.
Constraints: keep name, green identity, Agri character, light default, every route/control/i18n key/threshold logic; ≥48px targets (56–64 preferred); sunlight contrast; reduced-motion honored; offline-first.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The familiar farm dashboard at Weather-x-JioPhone fidelity: glanceable status, one-tap voice everywhere, oversized targets. It refuses novelty — no new navigation model, no decorative world; trust comes from recognition, and anything needing literacy is a failure.

OWN-WORLD: Light paper-green ground, white 18px cards on neutral hairlines, one neutral elevation reserved for the primary card; one committed green header shell; marigold and red strictly for warn/bad states; Plus Jakarta Sans display with system body; tabular giant numerals; 56-64px touch targets; speaker affordance on every reading.

STORY: The farmer opens and sees what needs doing within seconds — a count plus plain words — taps once to hear it or asks Agri aloud, and finds the pump already handled watering. Understands the field's state, believes the app speaks their language, does the next chore or nothing at all.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home ships the contract: green header naming field, crop and stage; Agri voice card with 60px play; amber count strip; 2x2 status cards with giant numerals and plain-word badges, each opening a sheet that reads itself aloud; big Ask Agri action over a standard bottom nav. No banners that shout, no rows that whisper.

FORM: Canon standing exit, seed key 9bcad2ba. Declined challengers survive only as build discipline: guide-word header (lexicon), one reading leads each screen (catalog), two-field halves switch (j-card), advice never covers data (orienteering).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
