    Set up ESP32 Wi-Fi

    1. Power on the rover. If its saved Wi-Fi is unavailable, join FarmRover-Setup in your device’s Wi-Fi settings.
    2. For the supplied sketch, FarmRover-Setup is open. Join it and open the Wi-Fi portal. 192.168.4.1 ↗
    3. Choose your network and save it in the portal. Then reconnect your phone or computer to its usual Wi-Fi and return to EnviroOS.

    The portal closes after 3 minutes. If setup timed out, restart the ESP32 and try again.

    This sketch provides MQTT readings in the app. Pi controls require the separate USB bridge firmware.
